# NEON DRIFTER

**Movement is survival.**

A web-based, touch-first arcade driving experience about flow, risk, and surviving the neon-lit streets.

## Quick Start

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
2. Click **START RUN**
3. Survive as long as you can

## Controls

### Touch (Mobile/Tablet)
- **Swipe left/right** - Change lanes
- **Hold anywhere** - Boost

### Desktop
- **Arrow keys** or **Mouse** - Change lanes
- **Space** or **Shift** or **Click** - Boost

## How to Play

You are a courier moving through hostile traffic.

- **Avoid obstacles** - Cars, vans, and police vehicles
- **Manage boost** - Hold to go faster, but energy drains
- **Control heat** - Crashes raise your wanted level
- **Survive** - Too many crashes end your run

## Design Philosophy

Neon Drifter is about **flow**, not winning.

Every mechanic supports uninterrupted motion. Risk creates style. The city doesn't care if you make it.

Short sessions. High intensity. Pure atmosphere.
